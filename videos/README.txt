🎬 HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN VIDEO:
==============================

Method 1 (Easiest - Directly on the website):
1. Open index.html in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, etc.).
2. Open the envelope and scroll to the "Our Sweetest Moments" video slot.
3. Click the "🎬 Replace Video" button on the player!
4. Select your video file (.mp4, .mov, .webm) from your computer or phone!
5. Click "Save Video ✨" — your video will load and play right away!

Method 2 (Using this folder):
1. Copy your video into this 'videos/' folder.
   Name it: sweet_moments.mp4
2. Open 'config.js' in a text editor.
3. Under the 'video' section, set:
   url: "videos/sweet_moments.mp4",
4. Save config.js and refresh index.html!
