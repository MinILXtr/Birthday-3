🎵 HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN BACKGROUND SONG:
=========================================

1. Choose a song she loves (like your couple song or her favorite romantic song).
2. Save the audio file into this 'music/' folder as:
   song.mp3
3. Open index.html in your browser, and it will automatically play your song!

Note: Even if you don't add an MP3, the website already has a built-in sweet romantic music-box melody synthesized with Web Audio, so music will work right away regardless!
