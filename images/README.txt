📸 HOW TO ADD YOUR PICTURE:
============================

Method 1 (Easiest - Directly on the website):
1. Open index.html in your browser (Google Chrome, Edge, Safari, etc.).
2. Open the envelope and scroll to the "Our Sweetest Moments" picture slot.
3. Click the "📷 Replace Photo" button directly on the photo frame!
4. Drag and drop your favorite picture or select it from your device.
5. Click "Save Picture ✨" — Done! The website will automatically save and remember it!

Method 2 (Using this folder):
1. Copy your picture into this 'images/' folder (for example: photo.jpg).
2. Open 'config.js' in a text editor (like Notepad or VS Code).
3. Under the 'photo' section, set:
   url: "images/photo.jpg",
4. Save config.js and refresh index.html!
